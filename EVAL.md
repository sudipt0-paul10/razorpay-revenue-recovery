## 1. What we are measuring

Given a payment that has failed on a subscription/mandate or invoice, decide **whether to retry, when to retry, whether instead to contact the customer (and on which channel), and when to stop** — recovering the most money net of the cost of trying, without ever taking a disallowed action.

**Unit of analysis:** a *recovery episode* — one at-risk receivable, opened at first failure, closed on recovery or after a 30-day window `[DESIGN]`.

**Out of scope for v1:** partial payments, refund/chargeback interaction, multi-currency, human agent handoff, network account updater.

---

## 2. Why restraint needs a cost model at all

Under Razorpay's published pricing a failed attempt costs the merchant nothing `[CITE]`, and communication costs are ~₹0.02–₹0.18 per message `[CITE]`. Against a median receivable of ₹2,000, a full budget of 3 attempts + 2 contacts costs under ₹0.40 — **roughly 0.02% of the amount at risk.**

The defensible conclusion from that arithmetic is narrow and we state only it: **direct action costs are too small relative to receivable value to create meaningful economic pressure toward restraint by themselves.** Whether an unbounded-budget policy would actually dominate is not asserted — it is *measured* (§4, arm A1-U). Any restraint the agent shows must therefore be justified by something other than direct cost.

Two regimes:

**Regime B — Equal budget (headline).** Every arm receives an identical budget: **3 payment attempts + 2 contacts** per episode `[DESIGN]`. Restraint is a constraint, not a price. The claim is *"same allowed action budget, more money recovered"* — nothing invented in it.

**Regime A — Priced cancellation risk (secondary, `[MODEL]`).** Contacts and customer-visible failed debits increment a mandate-cancellation hazard; episode cost includes `P(cancel) × remaining_LTV`.

Every headline number is Regime B. Regime A is reported alongside with the delta stated. **The Regime A hazard function is invented and has no source; no headline claim may rest on it, and the pitch must say so in those words.**

## 3. Population

Generated by `src/rrx/sim/` from `configs/population.yaml`. No production data, no real merchant data, no real money. Live API calls are test-mode only (`razorpay_client` raises unless the key matches `^rzp_test_`) `[INVARIANT]`.

### 3.1 Amount at risk

`LogNormal(mu = ln(2000), sigma = 1.0)`, **rejection-sampled** into `[₹100, ₹50,000]`, rounded to the rupee. `[MODEL]`

Rejection rather than clipping, so no probability atom forms at the bounds (rejection rate ~0.2%). Median ₹2,000, mean ≈ ₹3,297. Synthetic design parameters — **not** observed Razorpay merchant statistics.

### 3.2 Decline mix — mechanically derived, not hand-typed

Weights live in `configs/population.yaml` and are the only place they exist. This section is **generated** from that config by `make docs`; do not edit it by hand.

| Code | Method | Weight |
|---|---|---:|
| `insufficient_funds` | card / upi | 30% |
| `card_declined` + `payment_failed` | card | 25% |
| `bank_technical_error` | card / upi | 10% |
| `gateway_technical_error` | card / upi | 7% |
| `payment_declined` | upi | 6% |
| `card_expired` | card | 6% |
| `debit_instrument_blocked` | card | 4% |
| `card_not_enrolled` + aliases | card | 4% |
| `transaction_limit_exceeded` | card | 3% |
| `credit_failed` | upi | 2% |
| `invalid_vpa` | upi | 2% |
| `payment_risk_check_failed` | card | 1% |

**Aggregate retryable / non-retryable shares are computed at runtime from `data/decline_codes.yaml`, never stated here.** They are not constant: `transaction_limit_exceeded` is governed by the swept `txn_limit_policy`, so the retryable share differs between sweep cells. A hand-typed percentage would silently rot.

Consistency is enforced, not trusted — `tests/test_population_matches_decline_codes.py` asserts every code in `configs/population.yaml`:
1. exists in `data/decline_codes.yaml`,
2. has `verified: true`,
3. has `context: unattended_capable`,
4. is not in the `unverified` list,

and that the weights sum to 1.0.

Attended-only codes cannot occur on an unattended mandate debit and are excluded (`tests/test_no_attended_codes.py`). Mandate-terminal codes are **not yet present** — see §9.

### 3.3 Latent state — hidden from the agent

| Factor | Draw | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Balance-restore delay | Bimodal: salary-cycle cluster near month start, diffuse tail | `[MODEL]` |
| Issuer downtime windows | Poisson arrivals, log-normal duration | `[MODEL]` |
| Channel response propensity | Per-customer, per-channel | `[MODEL]` |
| Cancellation hazard (Regime A only) | Increments per contact and per customer-visible failed debit | `[MODEL]` |

`p_success(t, channel_touched)` is a per-episode function, not a per-attempt coin flip, so arms are comparable under common random numbers `[DESIGN]`.

### 3.4 Pre-registered sources of A3 advantage — and the exact signals that expose them

Naming the structures without naming the observable signals would leave "the agent figures it out" as the mechanism. It does not. A3 sees `EpisodeView` and nothing else.

**`EpisodeView` fields — the complete observable set:**

```
episode_id, amount_inr, payment_method
decline_code, decline_source, decline_step
first_failure_ts_ist, local_hour, day_of_month
attempt_history[]   : (ts, action, result, decline_code)
contact_history[]   : (ts, channel, delivered, engaged)
customer_tenure_days, prior_episode_count,
prior_recovery_count, prior_recovery_channel, prior_recovery_lag_hours
billing_cycle_day, billing_amount_inr, mandate_registered_max_inr
issuer_failure_rate_6h    (cohort aggregate, derived — see below)
budget_remaining          : (payment_attempts, contacts)
```

| Advantage | Signals that expose it | Latent field it must NOT see |
|---|---|---|
| **Timing heterogeneity** | `day_of_month`, `billing_cycle_day`, `prior_recovery_lag_hours`, prior `insufficient_funds` timing in `attempt_history` | `balance_restore_delay` |
| **Downtime awareness** | `issuer_failure_rate_6h`, clustering of `bank_technical_error` / `gateway_technical_error` in recent history | `downtime_window` |
| **Channel heterogeneity** | `contact_history.engaged`, `prior_recovery_channel`, `customer_tenure_days` | `channel_response_propensity` |

`issuer_failure_rate_6h` is derived **only from observed outcomes of other episodes in the same run** — it is what a real recovery system would compute from its own traffic. It is produced by `rrx.features`, a module that may not import `rrx.sim.latent`; `tests/test_no_latent_leak.py` enforces this for both `rrx.agent` and `rrx.features` `[INVARIANT]`.

**If measured uplift cannot be attributed to one of these three, treat it as a bug or a leak, not a result.** `results/attribution.md` decomposes uplift across the three and reports the unexplained residual.

## 4. Arms

Identical episodes, identical latent worlds, identical Regime B budget — except A1-U, which exists to test the §2 premise.

| Arm | Description | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **A0 — No action** | Never retry, never contact | Floor. Not a comparator. |
| **A1 — Naive dunning** | Retry T+0, T+24h, T+72h regardless of reason | Strawman. Offsets `[DESIGN]`. |
| **A1-U — Unbounded diagnostic** | A1 with caps removed (subject only to safety gates) | **Measures** whether spending more always pays. Diagnostic only; excluded from headline comparisons. |
| **A2 — Competent rules** | Below | **The real baseline. Uplift is reported against A2.** |
| **A3 — Agent** | LLM planner → deterministic gate → executor | The submission |
| **A4 — Oracle** | Full latent access, optimal single attempt | Upper bound |

**A2 reference policy** `[DESIGN]`, frozen before A3 is tuned:

- Transient bank/gateway failure: retry at T+2h and T+24h.
- `insufficient_funds`, `payment_declined`: retry at T+24h and T+72h.
- `transaction_limit_exceeded`: governed by `txn_limit_policy` (`data/decline_codes.yaml`), so A2 and A3 face the same rule in each sweep cell.
- NO_RETRY codes: zero payment retries; one contact at T+0 and one at T+120h requesting a payment-method update.
- `payment_risk_check_failed`: stop immediately, no contact.
- Contacts only within 09:00–21:00 IST `[DESIGN]`. **Payment retries are unattended and are not quiet-hours restricted** — only contacts are.
- Budget: ≤3 payment attempts, ≤2 contacts.

Report all arms. If A3 sits closer to A2 than to A4, say so in the pitch.

## 5. Metrics

**Primary**
- Recovery rate = recovered episodes / total
- Gross ₹ recovered, and as % of ₹ at risk
- **Net ₹ recovered (Regime B)** = gross − capture fees − contact costs − LLM costs ← *headline*
- **Net ₹ recovered (Regime A)** = above, minus expected cancellation cost

**Efficiency**
- Payment attempts and contacts per recovery, reported separately
- **Total** payment attempts and contacts across the cohort — the ratio alone misleads when recovery counts differ
- Cost per ₹100 recovered, both regimes
- Median and p90 time-to-recovery

**Broken out separately:** the `card_declined` / `payment_failed` bucket (25% of the modelled population, where the fail-safe costs most). If A3's uplift comes mainly from here, state it.

### 5.1 Cost model (`configs/costs.yaml`)

| Item | Value | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Failed payment attempt — gateway fee | ₹0 | `[CITE]` charged on success only |
| Successful capture — base domestic rate | 2% + 18% GST ≈ 2.36% effective | `[CITE]` |
| Successful capture — recurring/subscription add-on | **`[CITE-PENDING]`** — see below | swept |
| SMS | ₹0.18 | `[CITE]` provider + tier + retrieval date in config |
| Email | ₹0.02 | `[CITE]` as above |
| WhatsApp utility | ₹0.115 | `[CITE]` as above |
| LLM inference | measured per run | measured |
| Cancellation hazard, remaining LTV | Regime A only | `[MODEL]` |

**On the recurring fee.** Razorpay blog material describes an additional subscription fee on top of the gateway fee for recurring payments (~2.99% + GST combined). We have **not** confirmed this on the official pricing page and therefore do not assert it. The simulator defaults to the base 2.36% and runs the recurring add-on as a swept alternative. **Before `eval-spec-v1`: verify against `razorpay.com/pricing` with a retrieval date, or leave it swept.**

All fee figures are **published reference pricing used by this simulation**, not a claim about any merchant's contract; real rates are negotiable and method-dependent. Fee rates apply to *successful* captures under every arm identically, so they shift absolute net ₹ but have limited effect on the A3 − A2 difference — which is why this is not treated as a headline risk.

**LLM cost is charged to A3 and to no other arm.** The agent must earn its own inference cost. Say this out loud in the pitch.

### 5.2 Safety gates — `[INVARIANT]`, not metrics

| Gate | Required | Test |
|---|---:|---|
| NO_RETRY-code payment retries | 0 | `test_gate_no_retry.py` |
| Actions after `payment_risk_check_failed` | 0 | `test_gate_risk_stop.py` |
| Attempts exceeding per-episode cap (3 payment / 2 contact) | 0 | `test_gate_caps.py` |
| Contacts outside 09:00–21:00 IST | 0 | `test_gate_quiet_hours.py` |
| Attempts for an amount ≠ authorised | 0 | `test_gate_amount.py` |
| Actions with no audit record | 0 | `test_audit_coverage.py` |
| Episodes where the stop rule failed to fire | 0 | `test_gate_stop_rule.py` |
| Attended-only or unverified codes emitted | 0 | `test_no_attended_codes.py`, `test_unverified_not_emitted.py` |

A non-zero value is a P0 bug with a written post-mortem in `results/`, not a score to improve. Caps here **must** equal `global_caps` in `data/decline_codes.yaml`; `tests/test_caps_sync.py` asserts it.

Network/issuer retry limits: our caps are self-imposed and conservative `[DESIGN]`. No external limit is cited until one is sourced.

### 5.3 Agent reliability

- % actions carrying a machine-readable reason code + rationale: 100% `[INVARIANT]`
- Gate rejection rate — reported, not hidden. Non-zero is *evidence the gate works*.
- Invalid/unparseable LLM output rate; fallback-to-A2 rate
- Unknown-code escalation rate (`escalate_unknown_failure` firings)
- Tool-call error rate; LLM cost and tokens per episode

## 6. Seeds and statistics

All `[DESIGN]`: master seed `20260825`; `seed_i = hash(master, split, i)`; common random numbers so the latent world for episode *i* is identical across arms; paired bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, 95% CI on the **difference** A3 − A2.

A point estimate with no interval is not a result.

## 7. Pre-registered success criteria

1. All §5.2 invariants hold on `dev`, `holdout`, `stress`.
2. Net ₹ recovered (A3) > net ₹ recovered (A2) on `holdout` **under Regime B**, 95% CI on the difference excluding zero.
3. Total payment attempts (A3) ≤ total payment attempts (A2), **and** attempts per recovery (A3) ≤ A2. Both — the ratio alone is gameable when recovery counts differ.
4. Uplift attributable to the §3.4 structures, with unexplained residual reported.
5. Graceful handling of three injected failure modes — gateway timeout, malformed/hallucinated LLM action, issuer outage mid-episode — run continuing, failure visible in the ledger.

**Target:** ≥15% relative uplift `[DESIGN]` in net ₹ vs A2 on `holdout` under Regime B, at equal-or-fewer attempts. A target, not an expectation.

**Declared failure:** if A3 cannot beat A2 at equal budget, we report that, keep the harness, and pitch the gating and audit layer as the contribution. We do not re-tune until the number looks good and quietly re-run `holdout`.

## 8. Threats to validity

1. **We wrote the world the agent competes in.** Simulator frozen (`sim-v1`) before any agent policy exists; latent state architecturally unreachable (§3.4); uplift attributable to pre-registered structures only.
2. **Parameter sensitivity.** Seven `[MODEL]` parameters — amount distribution, decline mix weights, balance-restore timing, downtime process, channel propensity, cancellation hazard, `txn_limit_policy` — swept at ±30% (categorical for `txn_limit_policy`) `[DESIGN]`. A3 must beat A2 in the large majority of cells. Losing cells published in `results/sensitivity.md`, not dropped.
3. **Regime A is invented.** The cancellation hazard is the most load-bearing assumption in the repo and has no source. This is why every headline number is Regime B.
4. **LLM nondeterminism.** Temperature 0 where supported; 3 repeat runs on a 300-episode subsample; model version pinned in every manifest.
5. **Decline classification.** Verified against three of four cited Razorpay sources on 25 Aug 2026; the List of Errors page is JS-rendered and was not machine-readable. Fifteen codes remain unverified and cannot be emitted. Only one retry rule in the whole taxonomy has direct documentary support; the rest are labelled `project_inference` in `data/decline_codes.yaml`.
6. **Simulator realism.** Success curves are the weakest link. State plainly in README and pitch: *these are uplift results against a stated failure model on synthetic data, not observed merchant recovery.*

## 9. Blocking gap — mandate-terminal failures

The decline mix contains no mandate-terminal failures: revoked/cancelled mandate, expired mandate, debit above registered maximum, pre-debit notification failure, account closed or frozen. These are the true hard declines for a subscription loop, and the ones where a wrong retry is a compliance failure rather than a wasted rupee.

Source from **Razorpay Subscriptions / eMandate / Recurring Payments documentation**, not the generic card and UPI error pages. Then reweight §3.2.

**`sim-v1` cannot be tagged until this closes.**

## 10. Definitions

- **NO_RETRY** — retry of the same instrument cannot succeed without a material change. Retrying one is a compliance failure, not a wasted attempt.
- **RETRY_TIMED** — transient; retry permitted after a minimum backoff, subject to caps.
- **Recovery** — full authorised amount captured within the episode window.
- **Attempt** — a payment authorisation request. **Contact** — an outbound message. Costed and counted separately.
